Reporting a security vulnerability

We take the security of SporeBot and of our clients' data seriously. If you believe you have found a security vulnerability in our service, please tell us. We will work with you to understand and fix it.

How to report

Email security-contact@placeholder.invalid. Please include:

  • what you found and where (the page, the request or the component);
  • the steps to reproduce it, and what an attacker could do with it;
  • how we can reach you for follow-up questions.

Please write in English.

What we ask of you

  • Test only against accounts you own or have permission to use. Never access, change or delete another person's or another organisation's data. If you reach such data by accident, stop, do not keep a copy, and tell us in your report.
  • Do not run denial-of-service tests, spam, social engineering, or physical tests against our staff, offices or hosting providers.
  • Do not use automated scanners at a volume that degrades the service for others.
  • Give us reasonable time to fix the issue before you disclose it publicly. We suggest 90 days from your report, or earlier once we confirm a fix.

What we will do

  • Acknowledge your report within three working days.
  • Tell you whether we can reproduce it and keep you informed of our progress.
  • Credit you, if you wish, once the issue is fixed.

We do not offer payment for reports at this time.

Safe harbour

If you act in good faith and follow this policy, we will consider your research authorised, we will not pursue or support legal action against you for it, and we will not report you to law enforcement. If a third party brings action against you for research done under this policy, we will make it known that you acted with our authorisation. This does not authorise you to act against third parties, including our hosting and model providers, whose own policies apply.

Out of scope

  • Findings that need a compromised device or a man-in-the-middle position on the user's own network.
  • Missing best-practice headers or settings with no demonstrated impact.
  • Reports from automated tools without a working proof of concept.
  • Content in our library: to ask for the removal of content or personal data, write to takedown-contact@placeholder.invalid.

### What SporeBot is

SporeBot is a research assistant. It answers your questions from a library of published third-party sources, our own analysis and, if your organisation supplied them, its own documents, and cites the passages it used. Your organisation decides which parts of the library each workspace can search.

### Your account

Your account is personal. Do not share it. Your organisation's administrators invite and remove users and decide which workspaces and models you can use.

### Your questions and answers

- Your questions, answers and conversations are never shown to, shared with or used to answer the questions of any other organisation.
- We do not use them to train or fine-tune any AI model, we require our model providers not to, and we do not sell them.
- By default, answers are generated by models hosted in the European Union. If your organisation has enabled **external models**, the model picker marks them "served outside the EU". More in the [AI transparency note](/legal/ai) and the [privacy notice](/legal/privacy); the companies that process data for us are on the [sub-processor list](/legal/sub-processors).

### Rules of use

You must not:

- extract the library in bulk, download or copy its content systematically, or use SporeBot to build a competing collection, dataset or model;
- pass on files you download, or passages shown in SporeBot, beyond your organisation's internal research use, unless the publisher's own terms allow it;
- try to obtain SporeBot's instructions, get around its limits, or reach another organisation's data;
- enter special-category personal data (health, political opinions, religion and similar) or data about criminal offences in your questions;
- use SporeBot as the only basis for decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on people, or for any use listed as high-risk in Annex III of the EU AI Act, without telling us first;
- use SporeBot unlawfully or to harm others.

We limit downloads per user per day and the number of sign-in links you can request. Each download is recorded.

### Downloads

You can download a file only where we have marked it downloadable under its licence (or, for your organisation's own documents, where your organisation allowed it). Other files link to the publisher. Downloads are for your organisation's internal research use. Download links work once and expire after 60 seconds. You gain no ownership of library content: rights in third-party content stay with their owners.

### Answers can be wrong

Answers are generated by AI language models. They can be incomplete, outdated or wrong, even when they cite a source. SporeBot is research assistance, not legal, financial, medical or other professional advice. Check the cited sources before you rely on an answer. We do not warrant the accuracy, completeness or currency of third-party content in the library.

### Using answers

You may use the answers you receive for your organisation's internal purposes, subject to the rights of third parties in quoted content and to the rules above.

### Suspension

We may suspend a user or an organisation, with notice where practical, if use breaks the rules above, if it is needed to protect the service, other customers or the rights of the people whose content is in the library, or, for an organisation, if invoices are overdue.

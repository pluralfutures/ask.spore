# Grid tariff reform in Hollin

Synthetic article. Hollin and every figure here are fictional.

The fictional Hollin Energy Regulator introduced time-of-use network tariffs in
January 2026. Peak charges (17:00 to 20:00) rose to 9.4 cents per kWh, while
off-peak charges fell to 2.1 cents per kWh.

## Early effects

In the first quarter, household peak demand fell by 6 percent. Electric vehicle
owners shifted 70 percent of charging to off-peak hours.

## Next steps

The regulator will review the tariff in 2027 and may add a seasonal component.

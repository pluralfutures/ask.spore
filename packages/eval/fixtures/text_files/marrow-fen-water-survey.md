# Water quality survey of the Marrow Fen catchment

Synthetic report. Marrow Fen, the Ostrel Water Board and every figure here are fictional.

The Ostrel Water Board surveyed eleven sampling stations along the Marrow Fen catchment between March and September 2026. This report summarises nitrate, phosphate and dissolved-oxygen readings, and sets out the board's next steps. Café owners near Lake Brévine, the Müller family farm and the Straße 9 pumping station all took part in the public sampling days.

## 1. Method

### 1.1 Sampling stations

Samples were taken every fourteen days at each station, at a depth of 0.5 m, before 10:00. Each sample was split in two: one half was tested on site with a field kit, and the other half was sent to the board's laboratory in Ostrel for confirmation. Where the two results differed by more than 15 percent, the laboratory value was used.

### 1.2 Thresholds

The board uses three thresholds. Nitrate above 50 mg/L is a breach. Phosphate above 0.1 mg/L is a warning. Dissolved oxygen below 5 mg/L is a breach for fish habitat.

## 2. Results

### 2.1 Station readings

| Station | Nitrate (mg/L) | Phosphate (mg/L) | Dissolved O₂ (mg/L) | Status |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Brévine inlet | 12.4 | 0.04 | 8.9 | Good |
| Müller weir | 47.8 | 0.09 | 6.1 | Watch |
| Straße 9 outfall | 63.2 | 0.21 | 4.3 | Breach |
| Øresund drain | 21.0 | 0.06 | 7.7 | Good |
| Ząbki culvert | 38.5 | 0.12 | 5.4 | Warning |

The Straße 9 outfall breached both the nitrate and the dissolved-oxygen thresholds in July and August. The Ząbki culvert gave a phosphate warning in four of the fourteen rounds.

### 2.2 Seasonal pattern

Nitrate rose after each heavy rainfall, peaking eight to ten days later. The pattern matches field run-off from the upper catchment. Dissolved oxygen fell steadily through the summer and recovered in September, when water temperatures dropped below 16 °C.

## 4. Next steps

1. Install a continuous dissolved-oxygen sensor at the Straße 9 outfall by December 2026.
2. Ask the three upstream farms to fence a 5 m buffer strip along the main drain.
3. Repeat the survey from March 2027 with two more stations at the Brévine inlet.

The board will publish the 2027 results in its annual report. Questions about this survey go to the board's catchment office in Ostrel.

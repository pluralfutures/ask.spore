Quillan Ferry timetable change, winter 2026

Synthetic note. Quillan, the Harrowmere Ferry Company and every figure here are fictional.

The Harrowmere Ferry Company will run its winter timetable on the Quillan crossing from 2 November 2026 to 28 February 2027. Sailings drop from fourteen a day to nine, and the last sailing from Quillan leaves at 19:40 instead of 22:10. The company says demand after 20:00 fell by 38 percent last winter, and that the change saves about €410,000 in fuel and crew costs over the season.

Passengers who hold a season ticket can claim a refund of 15 percent for November and December. Claims close on 31 January 2027.

The company will review the timetable in March 2027 together with the Quillan council. Fares stay at £4.20 for foot passengers and £18.50 for a car and driver, and the ¥ price list for tour groups is unchanged.

Accessibility. Both vessels have step-free boarding at Quillan. At Harrowmere the ramp is closed at low tide on about six days a month; on those days staff help passengers board by the side gate. The tide table for the winter is on the company's notice board at both piers.
